# Georgetown-HAAS Lower Level Entity Map

## Scope and Data Minimization

This map records verified dashboard-candidate entities from the approved, read-only area inventory. It excludes raw configuration-heavy and privacy-sensitive categories (including device trackers, images, buttons, events, remotes, automation definitions, and configuration controls) because they do not support the approved status-first Lower Level design and may contain personal or operational detail. Domain-level exclusion counts remain below so the inventory decision is auditable.

A `Selected` value of `yes` means the entity has a specific navigation, status, alert, environmental, everyday-control, or diagnostic purpose under the approved design. It does not authorize a dashboard write or service action.
